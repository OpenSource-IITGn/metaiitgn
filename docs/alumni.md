# Alumni at IIT Gandhinagar

The IITGN alumni network connects graduates across industries, geographies, and generations. Staying engaged with the alumni community helps current students with mentorship, career guidance, and professional connections, while alumni stay connected to the institute.

## Alumni Directory

The Alumni Directory is a searchable record of IITGN graduates, organized by graduation year, discipline, and current affiliation. It helps students find alumni working in fields of interest and enables alumni to reconnect with classmates.

Directory entries typically include:

- Name and graduation year
- Discipline (BTech/MTech/MSc/PhD)
- Current organization and role
- Location
- Areas of expertise or interest

Access to the directory is managed through the institute's alumni portal. Contact the alumni office for access details.

## Success Stories

IITGN alumni have gone on to pursue diverse paths — higher studies at top universities, roles in leading companies, entrepreneurship, research, and public service. Success stories highlight the range of careers alumni have built and the impact of their IITGN experience.

Common alumni pathways include:

- **Higher education**: MS, PhD, and MBA programs at universities worldwide
- **Industry roles**: Core engineering, software, finance, consulting, and analytics
- **Entrepreneurship**: Startups and ventures founded by alumni
- **Research and academia**: Research positions and faculty roles
- **Public service**: Civil services, policy, and development work

If you are an alumnus and would like to share your story, reach out to the alumni office.

## Mentorship

The alumni mentorship program connects current students with alumni who can offer guidance on:

- Course selection and academic planning
- Internship and job search strategies
- Higher studies applications (GRE, TOEFEL, statements of purpose)
- Career transitions and industry insights
- Entrepreneurship and startup advice

Mentorship relationships are typically one-on-one and run for a semester or longer, depending on availability and mutual interest.

## How to Stay Connected

- Register with the institute's alumni office after graduation
- Join the official IITGN alumni groups and networks
- Attend alumni events, reunions, and campus visits
- Volunteer as a mentor for current students
- Contribute to institute initiatives and student programs
