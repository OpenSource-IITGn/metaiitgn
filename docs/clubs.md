# Clubs at IIT Gandhinagar

Student clubs and hobby groups are a vibrant part of campus life at IIT Gandhinagar. They offer opportunities to explore interests beyond academics, build skills, and collaborate with peers across disciplines. This page lists the active clubs and how to get involved.

## Technical Clubs

| Club | Focus Area | Description |
|------|------------|-------------|
| **Metis** | Software Development | Development-focused club working on real-world projects, open-source contributions, and programming practice. |
| **Digis** | Digital Sports & Gaming | Game development, e-sports, and the study of digital sports ecosystems. |
| **Mean Mechanics** | Robotics & Automation | Designing and building robots, with hands-on experience in mechatronics and autonomous systems. |
| **Odyssey** | Astronomy & Space | Stargazing sessions, astrophysics discussions, and space technology workshops. |
| **GRASP** | Competitive Programming | Algorithmic thinking, coding contests, and problem-solving practice. |
| **Machine Learning Club** | Artificial Intelligence | Machine learning algorithms, data science projects, and AI research. |
| **Systems Club** | Systems & Networking | Computer systems, networking technologies, and hardware-software integration. |
| **Embed Club** | Embedded Systems | Microcontroller-based IoT projects and embedded systems development. |

## Student Organizations

Beyond technical clubs, IITGN has student-run organizations covering culture, sports, arts, and wellness. These include music, dance, drama, literary, and sports teams that organize events throughout the year.

## How to Join

Most clubs recruit at the start of each semester through the **Clubs Fair** and general body meetings. You can:

1. Attend the Clubs Fair (usually held in the first few weeks of the semester)
2. Reach out to club leads through the institute's student portal
3. Contact the Technical Council for guidance on technical clubs
4. Follow club social media pages for recruitment announcements

## Starting a New Club

If you have an idea for a club that doesn't exist yet, you can propose it through the student activities office. A new club typically requires:

- A core founding team
- A faculty advisor
- A stated purpose and activity plan

## Events & Activities

Clubs organize regular events including:

- Workshops and hands-on sessions
- Inter-college competitions and hackathons
- Guest lectures and industry talks
- Weekly meetings and peer-learning sessions
