# Contacts at IIT Gandhinagar

This page lists the key offices and points of contact at IIT Gandhinagar. Use these to reach the right department for academics, admissions, hostels, and other campus matters.

## Academic Offices

| Office | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Dean (Academic)** | Academic policies, curriculum, grading, and academic advisories |
| **Dean (Student Affairs)** | Student welfare, discipline, and campus life matters |
| **Registrar** | Admissions, student records, and official documentation |
| **Head of Department** | Department-specific academic queries (per discipline) |
| **Faculty Advisor** | Course selection and academic planning for your branch |

## Administration

| Office | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Director's Office** | Institute leadership and overall administration |
| **Registrar's Office** | Official records, certificates, and administrative services |
| **Finance & Accounts** | Fee payment, scholarships, and financial matters |
| **Establishment** | Staff recruitment, records, and HR administration |

## Student Services

| Service | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Hostel Office** | Accommodation, room allotment, and hostel facilities |
| **Mess** | Dining facilities and meal plans |
| **Health Center** | Medical services and student health support |
| **Counseling** | Mental health and personal counseling |
| **Placement Cell** | Internships, placements, and career services |
| **Alumni Office** | Alumni network, directory, and mentorship |

## Campus Facilities

| Facility | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| **Library** | Books, journals, and research resources |
| **Computer Center** | Computing resources and internet access |
| **Sports Complex** | Sports facilities and recreation |
| **Transport** | Campus transport and shuttle services |

## How to Reach

Most offices can be reached through:

- **In-person**: Visit the office during working hours
- **Email**: Official institute email addresses (listed on the IITGN website)
- **Phone**: Office landlines (available on the IITGN website)
- **Student Portal**: Many services are accessible online through the institute portal

## Emergency Contacts

For emergencies, contact:

- **Security**: Institute security office (24×7)
- **Health Center**: Medical emergencies during operating hours
- **Hostel Warden**: Hostel-related emergencies after hours
