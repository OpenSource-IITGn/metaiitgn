# Courses at IIT Gandhinagar

This page lists the courses offered at IIT Gandhinagar, along with guides and materials to help students plan their academics. Course offerings vary by semester — always verify current availability on the institute's academic portal before registering.

## Course Guides

Course guides give an overview of what each course covers, prerequisites, and recommended preparation. Use them to plan your semester load and understand what to expect before enrolling.

| Guide Type | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| **Course Catalog** | Full list of courses offered across disciplines |
| **Prerequisite Map** | Which courses to take before advanced ones |
| **Semester Plan** | Suggested course sequence by year and branch |
| **Elective Guide** | Open and departmental electives available |

## Course Materials

Course materials include lecture notes, assignments, readings, and reference resources shared by instructors. Access varies by course:

- **Instructor-distributed**: Shared directly by the course instructor in class or through the course portal
- **Library reserves**: Textbooks and reference books available at the institute library
- **Open resources**: Publicly available lecture notes and materials where instructors have chosen to share them

## How to Register

1. Review the course catalog for your branch and semester
2. Check prerequisites for each course you plan to take
3. Consult your faculty advisor or branch coordinator if unsure
4. Register through the institute's academic portal during the registration window
5. Attend the first class to confirm the course structure and evaluation scheme

## Tips for Course Selection

- **Balance your load**: Mix theory, lab, and project-based courses each semester
- **Check prerequisites**: Some advanced courses require specific prior courses
- **Plan ahead**: Certain courses are offered only in specific semesters
- **Talk to seniors**: Upper-year students can share insights on course difficulty and workload
