# Student Organizations at IIT Gandhinagar

IIT Gandhinagar has a vibrant ecosystem of student councils, clubs, and hobby groups that cover academics, culture, sports, technology, and wellness. Use this page to find the right community for your interests and connect with fellow students.

## Student Councils

Student councils represent student interests and organize institute-wide events in their respective domains.

| Council | Focus Area | What They Do |
|---------|------------|--------------|
| **Technical Council** | Technology & innovation | Organizes hackathons, technical workshops, and student-led technical initiatives. Oversees technical clubs and open-source projects. |
| **Cultural Council** | Arts & culture | Plans cultural festivals, music, dance, drama, and literary events across the academic year. |
| **Sports Council** | Athletics & wellness | Manages sports tournaments, fitness activities, and inter-college athletic competitions. |
| **Alumni Council** | Alumni relations | Connects current students with alumni, runs mentorship programs, and organizes reunions. |
| **Hostel Council** | Residential life | Represents hostel residents, coordinates mess services, and organizes hostel events. |

## How to Get Involved

Most clubs and councils recruit at the start of each semester through:

1. **Clubs Fair** — Usually held in the first few weeks of the semester. Meet representatives from all clubs and councils in one place.
2. **General Body Meetings** — Attend a club's GBM to learn about their activities and membership process.
3. **Student Portal** — Reach out to club leads and council representatives through the institute's student portal.
4. **Social Media** — Follow club and council social media pages for recruitment announcements.

## Starting a New Club or Council

If you have an idea for a club or council that doesn't exist yet, you can propose it through the student activities office. A new organization typically requires:

- A core founding team
- A faculty advisor
- A stated purpose and activity plan
- Approval from the appropriate institute authority
