## Types of Announcements

Updates at IITGN typically cover:

- **Academic calendar changes**: Semester dates, registration windows, exam schedules
- **Admissions**: New program announcements, application deadlines
- **Events**: Tech fests, cultural fests, guest lectures, workshops
- **Placements**: Company visits, placement drives, internship opportunities
- **Infrastructure**: New facilities, hostel allotments, campus improvements
- **Student activities**: Club recruitment, sports events, cultural programs

## Where to Find Official Announcements

For the most current and authoritative information:

- **IITGN official website**: https://www.iitgn.ac.in/
- **Institute notice boards**: Physical boards across campus departments
- **Student portal**: Academic and administrative announcements
- **Department notice boards**: Discipline-specific updates
- **Hostel notice boards**: Hostel and mess-related announcements

## Subscribe to Updates
